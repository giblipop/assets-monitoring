Choose a workbook

The preview shows each worksheet’s cell layout. All data rows and columns are included by default; adjust the crop if the sheet has title or footer rows.

Merged headings are recognized, and blank, repeated, or numeric headings are converted to safe unique column names. Embedded PNG, JPEG, GIF, and WebP pictures are attached to their worksheet cells; web hyperlinks (including links to images and PDFs) remain clickable. Local file links are retained as text because the browser cannot read files elsewhere on your computer. Up to 50,000 data rows per sheet and 100,000 rows per workbook are imported in background batches.
